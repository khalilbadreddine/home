# The Black Ships — History of Japan (30 s sample)

In the year 794, Japan's emperor moved into a brand new capital. Kyoto. And the emperor would live there for more than a thousand years. For over two centuries, the shoguns kept Japan closed to the outside world. Then, in July 1853, four black ships steamed into Edo Bay. They breathed smoke. They carried cannons. And within fifteen years, the old Japan was gone.
